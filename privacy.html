Privacy Policy

Ironclad, Ironclad Free and Ironclad Free in the browser · Effective 16 October 2026

In short:

  • Ironclad (the paid Android app) collects no personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't use the internet.
  • Ironclad Free shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to serve them, as described below. The game itself still collects nothing.
  • The browser version (Ironclad Free in your browser) has no ads and collects nothing. GitHub, which hosts it, keeps standard server logs.
  • We never sell or share your data, because we never receive any.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Android apps Ironclad (com.projectmtb.game) and Ironclad Free (com.projectmtb.game.free), and the free browser version at rblizz24.github.io/Ironclad-free. All three are made by Ironclad's independent developer, called "we" and "us" here.

What the game keeps, and where

Your progress is saved on your device only:

  • What's saved: your Requisition (REQ), unlocked upgrades and vehicles, feat progress, best scores, music and sound settings. In Ironclad Free, also the number of ads watched today.
  • On Android it lives in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.
  • In a browser it lives in that browser's local storage for this site.

The game doesn't send this anywhere. We run no servers, and there are no accounts, sign-ins, analytics or crash reports. We never see your save.

If Android's backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's save in your device backup to your Google account. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy.

Android permissions

  • Vibration (both apps), for hit feedback.
  • Internet, network state and advertising ID (Ironclad Free only). Google's ads SDK uses these to load and show ads. The paid app requests none of them.

Ads in Ironclad Free

Ironclad Free earns its keep with rewarded ads: an ad plays only when you tap Watch an ad, and it pays in-game REQ. The ads come from Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK is built into the app and automatically collects the following, sent straight to Google:

IP address
Used to estimate your device's general location, such as your country or region. The app never uses GPS or precise location.
Device identifiers
Your device's Android advertising ID and app set ID.
Ad and app interactions
For example app launches, taps on ads and ad video views.
Diagnostics
Performance information about the app and the SDK, such as launch time and hangs.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, keep statistics and prevent fraud. It is encrypted in transit (TLS). We don't receive it. AdMob only gives us totals such as ads shown and earnings, which identify no one.

Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Ads are optional. You can play without ever watching one.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time from the title screen: Privacy › Ad choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads on most phones). Without one, Google can't personalise ads to you.

The browser version

The browser version is the free edition. It shows no ads and runs entirely on your device: its fonts and graphics come from the same site, and it calls no other services. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to run and secure the service, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don't receive those logs.

Children

Ironclad is a combat game and isn't directed at children under 13, or the higher minimum age where you live. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell, rent or share personal data, because we don't collect any. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's ads SDK sends to Google in Ironclad Free, described above.

Security

Your save stays inside Android's app sandbox, or your browser's storage for this site. Nothing is stored on our side. Google's ads SDK encrypts what it sends.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your game save stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app removes it, as does clearing its storage (Settings › Apps › Ironclad › Storage › Clear storage). In a browser, clear this site's data.
  • Ad data is kept by Google under its own retention policy. You can delete your advertising ID as described above, and manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.
  • We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. We'll still answer any question you send.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
