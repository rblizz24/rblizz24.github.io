# rblizz24.github.io

The Ironclad: Battle Tank website, served by GitHub Pages at <https://rblizz24.github.io/>.

- `index.html`: the landing page (play the free edition in a browser; Google Play listings).
- `privacy.html`: the privacy policy for Ironclad, Ironclad Free and the browser version. Both Android apps link here from **PRIVACY** on the title screen, and it is the address given to Google Play.
- `app-ads.txt` (added once AdMob issues its line): AdMob's authorised-sellers file, which must sit at the root of this domain.

The free browser version itself lives in [rblizz24/Ironclad-free](https://github.com/rblizz24/Ironclad-free).
